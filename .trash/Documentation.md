## Goal
**Main Grading**
Develop an AI that uses state machines or some other form of AI that runs in your game in an engaging or noticeable way. Anything other than this will not be grades such as aesthetic, assets, story, etc...

**Reflection**
In addition there will be a reflection brief you must provide a detailed report of the challenges you experienced an the ones you overcame (if any).

**Video Presentation**
We must also present an explanation of the game, giving credit to what others did and what you did in 3-4 minutes. Be efficient, have a script. Plan out what NEEDS to be explained and what NEEDS to be said about each item.

Any type of game that does not lend itself to AI systems such as something like Minecraft but without NPC's would be banned. More examples are deterministic games such as the following:
- Chess
- Draughts
- Othello

## Game Idea
The main idea is an arena game where the main character must deal with a variety of enemies based on a similar AI. The enemies include:

**Basic AI**
1. Archer (ranged attacks, prefers bows)
2. Swordsman (most defence, strategic damage, prefers swords)
3. Macebearer (strongest, rarely backs off, prefers maces)

**Bosses**
1. Flatula (joke farting boss, high health, prefers smell)
2. The Revolutionary (high health, high damage, slow. Allows for positioning)

**Main Mechanics**
The way you will engage in fighting these enemies is picking up items that will be generated by an arena spawner. This spawner will also have an AI. When maybe there is less than a certain amount of items on the map it will drop a random item for the player OR the AI to use during the fight.
