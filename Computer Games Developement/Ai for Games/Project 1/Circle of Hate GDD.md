## **Genre:**  
Arena, PvE, fighter

## **Game Elements:**

- Variety of melee and ranged weaponry.  
- Ability to quick swap weapons for freshly spawned ones  
- A spawner entity for weapon pool  
- Different personalities of AI  
- Attack patterns  
- Boss enemy  
- (possible) environmental effects

### **Player Count:**  
Single player game.

### **Tech Form:**

## **Gameplay**

# **Overview:**  
A third person perspective medieval combat game.  
The player is dropped in a gladiator ring and put up against increasingly harder combinations of enemy types until reaching a boss round combining all the personalities of the previous AI’s.  
The combat is balanced around the player being able to read/understand the choices available to the AI type they’re up against and play accordingly.  
This gameplay loop allows the player to learn with each round how the AI behaves and eventually master fighting each one\!

## **Gameplay outline:**

- Run the executable  
- Pass the main menu  
- Select difficulty  
- Opening cut scene showing the arena  
- Move around the arena and fight  
- Win a round and move on to the next  
- Lose a round and use 1 of 1 continues to try again  
- Beat the boss to end the game  
- Completing challenges listed in the pause menu grants the player more earnings/score in a round

The gameplay loop keeps people playing as the challenge of learning what the AI can and can’t do means you can quickly master the mechanics and breeze through the game.  
The added challenges provide re-playability and number-go-up mentality.

## **Key Features:**  
Diverse combat, the player has to make even more choices than all of the AIs combined. They have to adapt to the AIs decisions and challenges presented in each level.

Weapon variety, the difference in weapons available to the player at a given time really changes how the game is played with more defensive, offensive or ranged play styles depending on the challenge ahead.
