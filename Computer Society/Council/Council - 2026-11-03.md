**Clubs and Society Policy**
Original policy was made in 2007 and amended in 2010. Has too much emphasis on sport and elite levels specifically. New amendment is to focus on more public usage instead of professional international sport.

If drama wants to bring in a new instructor for instance they will have more ability to do so. Coaching sessions should be more open.

In addition the budget for such hiring and sessions in 2007 does not match the modern economy. **8500 euro** is the new maximum but this does not mean they need/get 8500. It also cannot be used on anything other than coaching, however if the coach is smart they may put that in the payment of their visit as part of the coaching.
